## \# HR Analytics - Employee Attrition Analysis

## 

## \## Project Overview

## 

## This project analyzes employee attrition using MySQL and Microsoft Excel. The objective is to identify the key factors contributing to employee turnover and provide actionable business recommendations to improve employee retention.

## 

## \---

## 

## \## Business Problem

## 

## Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations as it increases recruitment costs, training expenses, and productivity loss. The HR department wants to understand:

## 

## \- Which department has the highest attrition?

## \- Which job role experiences the highest turnover?

## \- Does overtime impact employee attrition?

## \- Which age group has the highest attrition?

## \- What recommendations can improve employee retention?

## 

## \---

## 

## \## Project Objective

## 

## To analyze employee attrition using SQL and Excel, identify patterns affecting employee turnover, and provide data-driven recommendations for HR decision-making.

## \## Key Business Insights

## 

## \- Sales department recorded the highest employee attrition.

## \- Employees working overtime showed higher attrition than employees who did not.

## \- Employees aged 26–35 experienced the highest employee turnover.

## \- Lower job satisfaction was associated with higher attrition.

## \- Research \& Development had the lowest attrition rate.

## 

## \---

## 

## \## Business Recommendations

## 

## \- Reduce overtime in departments with high attrition.

## \- Improve employee engagement initiatives.

## \- Review compensation for high-turnover job roles.

## \- Strengthen onboarding and mentoring programs.

## \- Promote work-life balance initiatives.

## \- Conduct regular employee satisfaction surveys.
