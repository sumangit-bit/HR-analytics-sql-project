## HR ANALYTICS EMPLOYEE ATTRITION ANALYSIS



#### Executive Summary



This project analyzes employee attrition using SQL and Microsoft Excel. The objective is to identify factors influencing employee turnover and provide data-driven recommendations to improve employee retention. SQL was used for data cleaning and business analysis, while Excel was used to build Pivot Tables, KPI cards, and an interactive dashboard.



#### BUSINESS PROBLEM



Employee attrition is one of the biggest challenges faced by organizations because replacing employees increases recruitment costs, training expenses, and productivity loss.



The HR department wants answers to questions such as:



* Which department has the highest attrition?
* Which job role experiences the highest turnover?
* Does overtime increase employee attrition?
* Which age group leaves the company most frequently?
* What recommendations can reduce employee attrition?



The objective of this project is to analyze employee attrition using SQL and Excel, identify factors affecting employee turnover, and provide actionable recommendations to support HR decision-making.

#### BUSINESS INSIGHTS





Insight 1



Sales department recorded the highest attrition rate among all departments.



Insight 2



Employees working overtime experienced a significantly higher attrition rate than employees who did not work overtime.



Insight 3



Employees aged 26–35 showed the highest employee turnover.



Insight 4



Lower job satisfaction was associated with higher attrition.



Insight 5



Research \& Development had the lowest attrition rate.





#### BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS



Based on the findings:



* Reduce overtime in high-risk departments.
* Conduct employee engagement surveys regularly.
* Improve career development opportunities.
* Review compensation for high-turnover roles.
* Strengthen onboarding programs.
* Promote work-life balance initiatives.
* Implement employee recognition programs.



#### CONCLUSION



This project successfully analyzed employee attrition using SQL and Excel.



SQL was used to clean the data and answer key business questions, while Excel was used to present the findings through Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, and an interactive dashboard.



The analysis identified important factors contributing to employee attrition and provided actionable recommendations that can help HR improve employee retention and support better business decisions.
